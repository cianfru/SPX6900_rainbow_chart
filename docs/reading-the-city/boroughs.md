# The Outer Boroughs

Manhattan holds **1,693 lots**. There are currently **4,893 residents**.

The overflow builds in **Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Jersey City** — another 4,582 lots, laid on
the same street bearing as the island.

## Who moves

**The newest wallets are the ones who cross the river.**

Picking by tenure means nobody is displaced by a price move or a partial sale — only by having
arrived later than the people already on the island. A holder who has been here since launch keeps
their address regardless of what the market does.

The split falls out of the data rather than a rule: Manhattan's mean tenure runs about three times
the boroughs'.

## What the boroughs are not, yet

Turning the boroughs into the **other chains** — Brooklyn as Base, Queens as Solana — is designed and
would be the most striking thing the city could say. Base has more than twice Manhattan's population
while Manhattan holds roughly 94% of the value: all the people across the river, all the money on
the island.

It is **not built**, because there is no per-wallet data for Base or Solana. We have headcounts and
nothing else.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Rendering invented buildings for chains we cannot reconstruct would be exactly the kind of thing
this project does not do. Until a Base transfer extract runs through the same engine, the boroughs
stay overflow housing.
{% endhint %}
