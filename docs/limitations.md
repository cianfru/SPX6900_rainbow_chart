# Limitations

The city is a behaviour read. It is not a signal, and it is not advice.

## Wallets are not people

One person can hold several wallets. An exchange holds many people's. Never read the resident count
as a headcount of humans.

## The city is a top slice, not everyone

Ethereum, Base and Solana together hold roughly **230,000 wallets**. The city has **6,275 lots**.

In [SPX and Both modes](modes.md) the city is inherently a view of the largest holders. AEON mode,
at 3,333 tokens across 1,176 wallets, is the one mode where everyone fits.

## Street addresses mean nothing

[Districts](reading-the-city/districts.md), neighbours and lot positions are derived from a hash of
the wallet's own address. They are stable, and they are fiction.

## Flow is not intent

A red beam means coins left the wallet. It cannot distinguish a sale from a transfer between
someone's own wallets, and a deposit to an exchange is not yet a sale.

## Only Ethereum is reconstructed

Base and Solana holdings are not in the buildings. We have headcounts for those chains and nothing
per-wallet, so the [boroughs](reading-the-city/boroughs.md) stay overflow housing rather than
pretending to be other chains.

## Exchange coverage is what we have identified

The harbour shows **tagged** addresses. Binance and Coinbase genuinely hold almost nothing, which
was verified rather than assumed — so the venue split is representative rather than an artifact. But
it is always "of what we've identified".

## Height compresses

Two buildings of visibly similar height can differ substantially in tokens. The ordering between
them is always exact; the spacing is not linear. The real figure is one hover away.
